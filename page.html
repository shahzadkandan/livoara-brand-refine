        Best Practices For Beginners

        Best Practices for Beginners

        To get the most out of your CELORIVA™ Knee ULTRA, follow these best practices for optimal relief and long-term results. Consistency is key, and with just a few minutes each night, you can experience noticeable improvements.

        1. Prepare Before Use
        Fully charge the device before first use (Type-C charging; lasts 2–5 hours per charge).

        Ensure a snug fit by adjusting the straps for comfort and proper skin contact.

        Use on bare skin or over thin clothing to maximize the effectiveness of heat and vibration therapy.

        2. Optimal Usage Guidelines
        Turn on the CELORIVA™ Knee ULTRA by holding the power button for 2 seconds.

        Choose your therapy mode:
        Press the power button to switch between Infrared and Laser therapy.

        Adjust heat levels to your comfort: 105℉, 120℉, or 140℉.

        Choose your vibration setting: Low, High, or Automated Cycle.

        Limit each session to 7–10 minutes. The device will automatically shut off for safety.

        3. Evening Routine (Recommended Use)

        For best results, use the CELORIVA™ Knee ULTRA each night before bed:
        Run a 7–10 minute session on each knee to reduce pain, stiffness, and inflammation.

        This 3-step nightly routine is designed to help you wake up feeling looser, lighter, and more mobile.

        4. Best Times to Use
        Morning         – Loosen up stiff knees before starting your day

        After Exercise – Enhance recovery and reduce soreness

        Before Bed (Recommended) – Maximize healing during rest with the full CELORIVA™ system

        5. Safety & Maintenance
        Do not use on broken skin, infections, or severe injuries without medical clearance

        Clean the device regularly with a soft, slightly damp cloth (do not submerge)

        Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

        By following these best practices consistently, you’ll get the most out of your CELORIVA™ Knee ULTRA and feel the difference it can make in just 7 days.
