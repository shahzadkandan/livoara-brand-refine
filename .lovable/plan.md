# LIVOARA compact video-led product page

## Goal
Current LIVOARA design ko preserve rakhte hue `/product` ko RADA Beauty aur Vybes jaisa compact, media-rich aur purchase-focused banana, bina unki branding, media, ya unsupported claims copy kiye.

## Changes
- First product view ko tighter two-column layout mein refine karna so image/video gallery aur purchase information zyada jaldi visible ho.
- Existing gallery mein approved LIVOARA product videos add karna; images ka thumbnail-hover preview aur desktop zoom preserve karna.
- Product title, approved bundle prices, benefits, quantity aur purchase actions ko compact hierarchy mein arrange karna.
- Large countdown block ko slim inline offer timer banana aur bundle rows ki height/spacing reduce karna.
- Repeated shipping, returns, payment aur trust blocks ko ek concise post-purchase trust area mein consolidate karna.
- Product details, specifications, shipping, box contents aur guidance ko compact accordions mein organize karna.
- Static repeated benefit area ko short video-led product story mein convert karna using only correct LIVOARA vanity media.
- Reviews/comments, comparison table, mobile swipe behavior, sticky mobile purchase action, policies, and all existing approved prices remain available with reduced surrounding whitespace.

## Guardrails
- LIVOARA fonts, colors, header, navigation, product identity, cart behavior, and Shopify responsibility remain unchanged.
- No copied RADA/Vybes videos or images; their pages are visual references only.
- No invented ratings, stock counts, guarantees, delivery dates, specifications, or customer claims.
- No custom backend, checkout, inventory, order, or payment implementation.

## Verification
- Check gallery image/video switching, hover preview, zoom, bundles, quantity, timer, cart buttons, accordions, review sliders, and sticky mobile action.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts have no page-wide horizontal movement, excess blank space, overlaps, console errors, or build errors.
