# LIVOARA single-product luxury storefront

## Goal
Build a complete, responsive LIVOARA website around the one existing travel vanity product. The supplied banner remains untouched and becomes the prominent full-width homepage visual.

## Pages and navigation
- Create a shared editorial header and footer with routes for Home, Product, About Us, Contact Us, Shipping & Returns, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions.
- Keep the store single-product: every shopping link and product mention leads to the same LIVOARA product page.
- Add unique page titles and social descriptions for every page.

## Home page
- Use the exact uploaded banner image as the opening visual, without regenerating or altering it.
- Preserve the banner's embedded “Made for every moment.” message and CTA visually; make the CTA area lead to the product page without placing competing text over the artwork.
- On smaller screens, use an intentional crop that keeps the vanity product visible and provide a clear accessible Shop Now action directly after the banner.
- Follow with the requested sections in order: Travel, Gifting, Product/Lifestyle, Brand Story, Testimonials, Newsletter, and Footer.
- Reuse the same product imagery through carefully selected crops/details rather than inventing additional products or collections.
- Mark unavailable facts—testimonial quotes, customer names, and business claims—as clearly editable placeholder content rather than presenting them as real claims.

## Product page
- Create a distinct conversion-focused layout inspired only by the referenced page’s UX structure, while retaining LIVOARA’s own warm editorial identity.
- Include a responsive product gallery using the supplied product imagery, title, rating area, price area, benefits, quantity control, Add to Cart, Buy Now, and trust-information slots.
- Add organized Product Details, Specifications, Reviews, FAQ, and Shipping & Returns sections.
- Use clearly labeled editable placeholders for price, rating/review counts, specifications, policies, and any other facts not supplied.
- Implement an on-page cart drawer and purchase controls as a polished front-end experience; checkout remains a placeholder because no payment or product data was provided.

## Supporting pages
- About Us: restrained brand story layout with editable copy placeholders where facts are unknown.
- Contact Us: accessible contact form with a local success state and editable contact-detail placeholders.
- Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Shipping & Returns: readable legal/policy layouts with explicit placeholder fields instead of fabricated terms.

## Visual system and responsive behavior
- Define a warm blush, cream, charcoal, and muted metallic-gold token system; use elegant serif display type and clean sans-serif body type.
- Favor spacious editorial bands, fine rules, restrained corners, and subtle motion over generic cards or gradients.
- Ensure navigation, gallery, purchase controls, forms, accordions, and footer work cleanly on mobile and desktop.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and maintain accessible contrast, focus states, semantic structure, and image alt text.

## Validation
- Confirm every route loads and all navigation targets exist.
- Test the homepage and product flow at desktop and mobile sizes, including banner crop, cart controls, accordions, and contact/newsletter states.
- Check for visual overlap, browser errors, and successful project compilation.

## Assumptions
- The uploaded banner is the only approved product image and will be reused through responsive crops.
- Unprovided commercial/legal facts remain visibly editable placeholders.
- No live payments, order submission, email delivery, or persistent customer data will be added in this pass.
