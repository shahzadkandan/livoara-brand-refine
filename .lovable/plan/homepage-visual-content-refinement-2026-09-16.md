# Homepage visual content refinement

## Goal
Keep the current LIVOARA design unchanged while improving the requested homepage sections with clearer problem-to-solution storytelling, woman-led product-use videos, product-box category imagery, a reference-matched editorial banner, and more photo-led reviews.

## Changes
- Update “Sound familiar?” to show four visuals: two real-life problems and two LIVOARA solution scenes, with concise labels that make the problem/solution distinction clear.
- Replace the current product-only clips in “Designed to be used, beautifully.” with three professional woman-led LIVOARA use-case videos, while tightening the section spacing and improving video placement on desktop and mobile.
- Keep all eight Future Categories, but use LIVOARA product-box imagery across the cards, drawing from approved existing product assets rather than unrelated category photography.
- Rebuild the editorial banner directly below Future Categories to closely match the supplied reference: soft blush full-width scene, large left-aligned message and dark Shop Now button, compact product box on the right, and handwritten-style supporting line.
- Expand “Thoughtful details, noticed in real routines.” from three to eight photo-led review cards using clearly illustrative customer portraits. Keep sample-feedback labels and reserved rating treatment so no verified customer or rating claim is fabricated.

## Technical details
- Frontend presentation only; no backend, checkout, inventory, or payment changes.
- Generate new woman-led video and illustrative portrait assets where existing approved media cannot satisfy the request; do not change the hero image.
- Preserve semantic design tokens, approved policy wording, accessibility labels, contained mobile sliders, and all existing shopping links to `/product`.
- Verify spacing, media playback, horizontal containment, and desktop/mobile presentation.
