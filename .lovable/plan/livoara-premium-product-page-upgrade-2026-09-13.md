# LIVOARA Premium Product Page Upgrade

## Goal
Turn the existing product page into a premium, conversion-focused shopping experience inspired by Amazon’s clarity and Celoriva’s long-form storytelling, while keeping LIVOARA’s own luxury identity and vanity product.

## Changes
- Rebuild the first product view with a stronger gallery, review summary, product highlights, and a clear three-line price hierarchy: MRP, selling price, and savings/offer.
- Add a polished purchase panel with quantity selection, purchase actions, delivery/status information, and trust details.
- Add a customer media section using the current LIVOARA imagery as video-preview placeholders until genuine customer videos are supplied.
- Expand social proof with a ratings overview and India-focused Hindi/Indian English sample reviews, all clearly labelled as samples.
- Add long-form product storytelling, benefit proof, comparison, and FAQ sections without inventing specifications or guarantees.
- Keep all unknown prices, ratings, delivery promises, technical details, warranty claims, and customer media clearly marked as editable placeholders.
- Refine desktop and mobile layouts, including the existing sticky mobile purchase action.

## Technical Details
- Update only the existing `/product` page and roadmap; no checkout, database, or new routes.
- Reuse the existing three LIVOARA images, cart behavior, shared buttons, typography, and semantic color tokens.
- Use accessible gallery controls, review labels, video-placeholder controls, accordions, and table markup.
- Verify product interactions, desktop/mobile appearance, metadata, and final build state.
