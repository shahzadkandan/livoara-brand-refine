# LIVOARA Product Purchase Panel Refinement

## Goal
Make the product purchase area feel clearer and more premium than the reference while preserving LIVOARA’s luxury styling.

## Changes
- Replace the current flat price box with two stacked offer-choice rows: regular price and selected launch offer, using the confirmed ₹4,999 / ₹2,999 pricing and 40% saving.
- Keep quantity and purchase actions prominent, then redesign order progress as one connected three-step line.
- Add a polished payment card below the delivery line with India-relevant methods: UPI, RuPay, Visa, Mastercard, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and Cash on Delivery.
- Use restrained trust copy and avoid unsupported countdowns, delivery dates, or guarantees.
- Refine spacing and hierarchy on desktop and mobile without changing the rest of the page’s content.

## Technical Details
- Update only the existing `/product` page and roadmap.
- Reuse the existing shared buttons, semantic colors, typography, cart behavior, and icons.
- Implement offer selection accessibly with pressed states and keep the current cart quantity flow.
- Verify desktop/mobile layout, interactions, and final build status.
